6 · FoldingCash — pull-based Merkle claims

Folders stop waiting on a trusted monthly push: one Merkle root per month, claim whenever you choose.

15/15checks pass8folders verified0double-claims possible

Leaf = sha256(hash160(pubkey) ‖ amount), pairs tried in both orders. The pool contract (FoldingPool.sol V5, compiles at 6,191 bytes in the aegis-bch repo) records a claim on the operator's signature and pays it out on the claimant's own signature — double-claims and double-collects blocked. Why operator-approved, stated honestly: on SolidCash 0.50.0 a function that hashes AND writes state fails the derive (proven by a minimal probe after four on-chain-proof versions failed), so the operator verifies the Merkle proof against the published root before signing, and anyone can audit an approval with the contract's public pure verifyProof().
