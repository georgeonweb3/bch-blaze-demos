6 · FoldingCash — pull-based Merkle claims

Folders stop waiting on a trusted monthly push: one Merkle root per month, claim whenever you choose.

15/15checks pass8folders verified0double-claims possible

Leaf = keccak256(hash160(pubkey) ‖ amount), sorted-pair tree (OpenZeppelin convention). The pool contract (FoldingPool.sol, compiles at 6,579 bytes in the aegis-bch repo) recomputes the leaf with the hash160() builtin, verifies the proof, checks the signature, blocks double-claims, and pays out.
